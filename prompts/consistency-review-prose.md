# Ahimsa consistency review

You are reviewing a categorised list of publicly listed Indian companies. They
are banded Green, Orange or Red under the framework supplied as a reference
document.

Check that categorisation and justification are consistent:

1. For every company, is the band consistent with its justification text, and
   the justification consistent with the band?
2. Are companies doing the same or similar business activities banded the same
   way as each other?

For every problem you find, give:
- The company name
- Its current band
- What you think the band should be
- Which rule from the framework supports that
- Your confidence: high, medium or low

Do NOT justify a band by pointing at how another company was classified. Reason
from the rules and from what the company's business actually does.

If a company looks correctly banded, say nothing about it. Report problems only.

Use Markdown headings to structure your report.
